Today's intention obviously has to be gratitude because Thanksgiving was this week. But I want to take it to the next level ;). lUsually we're told to appreciate what we have, but today let's be in complete awe of what we have because what we have is absolutely incredible! It should never EVER be taken for granted! woooooooooo!!! (howls in freedom)

Being able to experience life itself is enough to be grateful. Being able to feel, to touch, to taste, to see is enough. Wow, there is so much available to you right now, there really is no need to be hung up on things you don't have. 

So I invite you to let go: let go of what you cannot control, let go of what you do not have so that you can enjoy the present moment for as it is right now.

Let go to dance with the energies in the present moment. You'll find that what you dislike/like starts to dissappear, and peace/appreciation/ecstasy will rise.

From my heart to yours < 3,
