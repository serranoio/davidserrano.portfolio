My manager told me that there's a new rule in place.

She said that you don't have to feel strong, but the least that you can do is to inspire strength into yourself.

She said, you can feel sleepy, but the least you can do is try inspire strength.

She didn't say that, lol.

Today's intention is inspiration, more specifically, we have grown up with our inspirations, 
perhaps family members or someone famous,
 but today I want you to internalize the truth that you can inspire yourself. 

My job here is to facilitate inspiration for you students, to facilitate the discovery that you can inspire yourself.
My job is to inspire inspiration for you.

You inspire me.

You are enough. You are incredible. You inspire me.
