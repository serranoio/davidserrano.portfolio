_These are the types of people who I surround myself around. There’s no room for mediocrity._

My friends are an eagle, a lion, a shark, elite in their own way, dominante in their habitat

Yet they are also a dove, a flamingo, a butterfly, gorgeous in their own way, with no need to dominate.

 > _They're elite and it does not consume them_

## Glorification

My friend group glorifies protecting their impressive abilities rather than throwing them away

> _This is glorifying staying in to focus on yourself instead of going out so much that you have no time for yourself_

My friend group glorifies putting in work rather than consuming shiny things that require no effort to get

> _This is glorifying working on a side hustle rather than glorifying going to a concert_

## Fullness

My friends are humble enough to let go of their titles to realize how much more potential they have

My friends sacrifice everyday pleasures to focus on building themselves because they know that they are so much more capable

My friends get the f*** back up because there's more to them than failure

## What We Are Not

It's not a free for all.

It's not toxic.

It's not every man for themselves.

It's the flame of everyone's soul that shines so bright that it makes your flame shine brighter.

## The Forever Support Circle

A support circle is typically only found for those who are low

Self-help books are marketed for those who are low

Therapy as well

But we have nothing for those who are average and want to level up!

Self-help, therapy and your suppoort circle die when comfort breeds complacency, but they never have to die.

Instead, it's a life long journey that never ends

It always has to be cultivated. Attend your garden.


## So tell me

When your friend group is talking about pleasures all the time, the next concert, the next party, meaningless bs, are they really giving themselves the chance to shine?


## My friend Group

This environment does exist as startup culture: ideas flourish, people bring out the best in each other, they push each other and they cheer themselves on.
