## The Totality of Life Ordained on Aeneas' Shield

 _Aeneas' shield was forged by the Fire God Vulcan, was delivered by hand by the Roman Goddess of Love, Venus, and it showcases illustrations of the history of Rome, such as Romulus & Remus, the founders of Rome, being nurtured by a wolf, and Julius Caesar standing triumph in victory to name a few.
In stark contrast, my shield was made by I don't know, and delivered by Amazon. This shield showcases the beautiful interplay of the 5 divine elements: Earth, Air, Fire, Water... And Aether in the center. I wrote a 'lil poem 'bout it._

What is it like to flow?\
in the west, they say it is the flow state\
in the east, they say the universe flows through you...\

Guess what!?? You are the universe and the universe flows through you\
It flows when we let go of our leaves.\
It flows when channel our energy to rise to the occasion like a phoenix.\
It flows when we embrace every aspect of life.\
It flows when we can finally see that this is our true nature.\
Just like how every petal contributes to the entire rose to make the rose so beautiful and full of life, Every petal of your life contributes to your life to make it so beautiful and full of life.\
Every petal, every day, every moment...\
They are all accessible to you only in the present moment.\
That is when we flow.
