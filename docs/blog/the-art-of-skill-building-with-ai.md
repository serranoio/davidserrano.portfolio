* Problem starts as something that is ambiguous and you have to conceptualize it. This is ONE conceptualization of an infinite amount. The best way to look at the problem space is to use Good Strategy Bad Strategy.


* The Problem Space & Solution Space are completely independent of each other. Do not conflate the two. Do not couple the solution to the problem. The reason is because you dont even have a mental model of the problem yet. The only way to get to the solution is to develop a singular mental model of the problem.
