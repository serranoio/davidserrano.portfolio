Problem starts as something that is ambiguous and you have to conceptualize it. This is ONE conceptualization of an infinite amount. The best way to look at the problem space is to use Good Strategy Bad Strategy.

The Problem Space & Solution Space are completely independent of each other. Do not conflate the two. Do not couple the solution to the problem. The reason why is because it blurs what the actual problem is. The only way to get to the solution is to develop a singular mental model of the problem. Then you can develop the solution space. Again, there is never one solution. There can be solution groups that point to the same architecture, mutually exclusive or inclusive solutions.


## Solution

### Put the shovel in the ground
1. To navigate this complexity, put your shovel in the ground to mark your **goal**

### Dig wide
Dig wide by giving a diagnosis. This diagnosis is firstly a brain dump. Then it lists out the constraints, limits, setbacks, nice to haves and prioritizes them.

### Reveal the problem space.
Afterwards, the problem becomes more clear, so you can formulate them into user scenarios & acceptance criteria.

### The Solution Space, derived from the problem space
Now you can conceive of solutions that fit all the criteria of the problem space
