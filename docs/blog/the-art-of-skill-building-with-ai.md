## Prompting

### Never affirm yourself to the AI.

I write down "I thought that the system was supposed to do X?" or "Isn't the system supposed to do X?", but then delete it and I rephrease it to: "what is X supposed to do". This is because the AI will favor your bias. Remove the bias and you will have an honest answer.

### Always question yourself: the adversarial review

I see this a lot on x, having an adversarial review. It is very worth having a separate eye on it. Your agent is going to be agreeable towards the solution that you have been coming up with it, getting eyees from another agent is valuable.

### The Problem Space & Its Separate Solution Space
Also, The Problem Space & Solution Space are completely independent of each other. Do not conflate the two. Do not couple the solution to the problem. The reason why is because it blurs what the actual problem is. The only way to get to the solution is to develop a singular mental model of the problem. Then you can develop the solution space. Again, there is never one solution. There can be solution groups that point to the same architecture, mutually exclusive or inclusive solutions. Sometimes people give solutions instead of problems. So please be sure to ask for the actual intention first so you know what should be done.

## Solution

### Put the shovel in the ground
1. To navigate this complexity, put your shovel in the ground to mark your **goal**

### Dig wide
Dig wide by giving a diagnosis. This diagnosis is firstly a brain dump. Then it lists out the constraints, limits, setbacks, nice to haves and prioritizes them.

### Reveal the problem space.
Afterwards, the problem becomes more clear, so you can formulate them into user scenarios & acceptance criteria.

### The Solution Space, derived from the problem space, oriented by a guiding policy
Now you can conceive of solutions that fit all the criteria of the problem space. Give a guiding policy that your solution should follow. It usually helps to give material examples for each part of the solution. These are user scenarios drawn generally, with an example, and acceptance criteria asserted.
