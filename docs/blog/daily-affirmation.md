> This is my daily affirmation as of November 5th, 2025

The only moment that you can exist in is the present moment\
 never the future\ 
 nor the past\
  so take in this moment for it is all you'll ever have <3.

Live this moment as if you are a samurai who can sense his entire surroundings\
- the challenge in front of him\
- his breath\
- his feet dug into the terrain\
But instead of your challenge being a fight, your challenge is to bask in teh entirety of life. \

Like how every little petal on a rose contributes to the whole to make a rose so beautiful and full of life, every little action that you make contributes to the whole of your life to make a life so beautiful and full of life.\

Every single step of life is a dance,\
the good steps and the bad steps.\
So don't just half ass it, lose yourself in the dance.\
