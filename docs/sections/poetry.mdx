# Poetry

A collection of words that flow like code.

---

## Digital Gardens

In silicon valleys where ideas bloom,
Algorithms dance in the quiet room.
Each function a petal, each class a stem,
Building cathedrals from logic's theorem.

---

## The Compiler's Lament

Syntax errors like fallen leaves,
Stack traces that the debugger weaves.
Through loops eternal we seek the light,
Until the tests all pass at night.

---

## Ode to the Golden Rose

Metallic petals catch the glow,
Of monitors in evening's flow.
A digital garden, carefully grown,
Where creativity has found its home.
