Preface

It was 8:11 am in a Summer in San Francisco in 2023. I had downloaded a personal project vacuum made by my mentor, [quobix](https://quobix.com/), onto my goland IDE.

 And that's all it took. I remember standing away from my desk in absolute awe of what I had just seen. I think this was the second epiphany moment of my lifetime. I saw code **forged** into art.

 It was the equivalent of the Stormbreaker forged by a star in Avengers: Infinity War. 

[[star]]

Every strike of the hammer, every drop of sweat, my mentor's +20 years of experience, of failing & learning, his entire soul had been poured into this codebase just as how the heart of the star had been poured into Stormbreaker's forge. Harnessing this amount of power has to be cultivated, learned.

I could feel the passion in the code. There is no reason why someone would build such a beautiful masterpiece if it didn't have passion behind it.

The axe is vacuum. The axe cuts, vacuum lints. Every part of the axe was consequentially made. The handle was created by Groot, which gives it an organic feel. To vacuum, this handle is the UX, the CLI, organic in its nature.

The codebase was free from enterprise bs. It was free from Conway's law. It had the nurturing required to reach its fullest potential.

To reach its fullest potential, the codebase didn't force the solution. it didn't lie to get to the solution. Instead, the solution already existed as a potentiality and the codebase only manifested the solution into it's physical form.

The beautiful rose did the same thing: came across the golden ratio to form its spiral. It's a very simple recursive formula that can be coded in like 5 lines. It chose this formula because it is optimal: allows for optimal sun exposure by minimizing pettal overlap. This is what truth discovery looks like in nature. What makes the rose beautiful is because it converged to truth. It didn't manufacture it. It didnt lie its way to the top. It didn't put itself inside a mask. Instead, it exists in its fullest expression, raw, exposed. It's the evidence of nature's passion.

This is what real engineering is. This is what I felt when I saw vacuum, this is what the rose represents.
