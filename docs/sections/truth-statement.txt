Preface

This has been created as a way to discover the truth. I would rather be wrong and discover the truth than right and have chosen a fantasy. What is bad makes space for what is good, and when you accept being wrong, you make space for the truth. That is a gorgeous truth of reality.

Just as how every petal of the rose contributes to the whole to make it so beautiful and so full of life, every day of your life contributes to the whole to make it so beautiful and full of life.

The golden ratio represents Truth. Nature had to let go of the ego in order to discover this formula.

Find and click a petal that is bouncy to read a poem!

All written by me.

This is the end, but you have just started your journey. There is always space for more.
