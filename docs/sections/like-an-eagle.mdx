# Like an Eagle (high low level)

When tackling new terrain, an eagle soars above the trees to get a high level view of the landscape
When tackling a new problem, you must soar above the lines to get a high level view of the system

Since only from the elevated perpsective can you spot your prey. And once your prey is spotted with your sharp eagle eyes, dive down deep into the code to catch the bug that was causing you trouble.

Bon apetit for the eagle, and problem solved for you.
