# Cooking a dish(MRs)

You add the base of the dish, and the majority of your code is written.

You add the seasonings to the dish, your finishing touches to the code.

Before cooking, you scoop your finger for a taste test, but really you should've been testing from the beginning ;).

You are satisfied with the taste & you put your code into an MR to cook. Once the pipelines turn green, it is done! It is ready to be consumed by other folks on your team. Make sure it doesn't stay too long in the MRcrowave or else it will become stale & will have to be rebased.
