# The UI & Backend Partner Dance

The UI asks for the backends hand (data), and the backend responds with its hand. The two follow a contract that allows them to move in harmony, in unison. 

The contract is the beat of the music that the UI and backend dance to. When the contract changes, both the UI & the backend will always step to the beat.

The two sway to product requirements, they move to the market, and together they make up a beautiful dance.
