# The Backstory

Ever since I moved to Silicon Valley, every single day I have spent coding on personal projects trying to make it big. Every day when I got home from work, I would immediately code. But no one ever gave a fuck. I had nothing to show for it anyway. I, as a creative, as a builder, respect the journey so much tho! To those out there that are building and you keep on failing, that to me is so much more incredible than flexing your vacations. This is because you believe in yourself. You show grit. You have passion.

At first, I tried to make a get rich quick mini app scheme. I built an app in a weekend. It was ass. I was burnt out. Then, I told myself real engineering only, I built a collaborative manuscript editor called Bookera using yjs, but I realized that that was so far out of my league as **I did not have the skills to make it scale**. I had to learn what [Conflict Free Replicated Data Types](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conflict-free_replicated_data_type) are on a Tuesday night when my friends are out here going out to the club. In September of last year, I gave up my glorified, perfect dream and just started F'ing around with agentic projects until March, all of which were made obselete by Claude Code's next release the week later. In March, I was like 'f it, it's time to make an automated trading bot. I don't care anymore about being a polished person who believes technical analysis is akin to astrology, it's time to just make money.

**In the age of AI, you can teach yourself literally anything.** I went ahead and used AI to give me a mental model about production trading bots. I was such a noob that I don't even know what a Sharpe ratio is. But I went to learn how Quants at Jane Street did technical analysis.

To those that have always believed in me, thank you. I appreciate you. However, I don't need anyone to believe in me. Respect has to be earned.
